·       Summarize the data youve been working with.

Your report should explain your findings in a way nontechnical stakeholders can understand and use.

 

Use the steps below to capture the required data and produce the analysis report.

 

  1. Begin by writing SQL commands to capture specific usable data for your analysis. You already preloaded the data you need into Codio.
  2. Specifically, the product manager wants you to complete the following analysis: 

 

A.      Analyze the number of returns by state and describe findings to include in your report.

o   Command: SELECT Customers.State AS STATE, COUNT(*) AS NUM-RETURNS FROM Orders INNER JOIN RMA ON Orders.OrderID = RMA.OrderID INNER JOIN Customers ON Customers.CustomerID = Orders.CustomerID GROUP BY STATE ORDER BY NUM_RETURNS DESC;

The above analysis provides the Quantigration product manager an insight into how many of their products are being sold to their customers within the United States and how many of those products are being returned to them for various reasons. This information is helpful in identifying trends in specific areas of the country and specifically the products themselves. Based on my findings, Massachusetts had the highest number of returns with 972 orders returned. Arkansas had the second highest number of returns with 844 orders returned. And Oregon had the third highest number of returns with 840 orders returned. I also noticed there are two states that have no returned orders from Quantigration. Specifically, Alaska and Virginia. This could be caused by a potential flaw in the data or possibly that these States did not return any ordered products during the last quarter before this RMA review.

 

The percentage of returns by product is a bit concerning when the top 3 percentages are all for the same product and it is also the companys top-selling product based on last quarters results. The top return percentage was for the 48 port Basic Switch. The reason was for being incorrect, which is at almost 8%. I find this reason to be very broad in the sense that being incorrect could mean many things. This is a design flaw and should be corrected to be more specific. Incorrect description, incorrect part sent or ordered by mistake. By doing so, it would make this clearer as to whether Quantigration needs to implement changes to their product descriptions, their shipping processes, etc.

 

The reason for the defects should also be investigated when it has the second highest percentage of returns at over 7%. This information should be flowed down to the production managers and the key suppliers of the 48 port Basic Switch to assess their processes and ensure their production lines are following procedures. If issues are found, corrective actions should be generated against the findings to understand the route causes, how the issues were missed and the changes to be implemented. By doing so, this should curb the number of returns for defects on this product.

 

The third highest percentage of returns was for other at just under 7%. Other is also another vague reason and should have more of an explanation. I would suggest making a change here as well. If the customer had the opportunity to express their reasonings for their return it would be clearer to Quantigration as to what changes they may need to make, if any, to the 48 port Basic Switch to better suit their customers needs.

 

Although the results of this quarterly review are concerning, I would recommend future analyses show how the product was ordered and its shipping details. This will allow another perspective to be looked at. If it was ordered online or bought in store. If bought online, who was the shipping carrier and their details? If it was bought in store, which store? And how was it delivered? This information would be beneficial in identifying trends in how the products are being handled outside of the manufacturing facility before customers have access to them. Providing shipping details is one of the many ways to know if the packages were damaged upon delivery. This perspective would give Quantigration more awareness into whether they need to make changes to their courier services.
